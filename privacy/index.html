PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: May 2026

At LEDGR, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

WHAT WE COLLECT

  • Account data: email address, username, password (encrypted)
  • Pick data: the sports picks you post publicly on LEDGR
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used (anonymized)

HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

  • To create and manage your LEDGR account
  • To display your picks and stats on the platform
  • To send you important account notifications
  • To improve the LEDGR platform

WHAT WE DON'T DO

  • We never sell your personal data to third parties
  • We never share your email without your consent
  • We don't use your data for advertising

DATA STORAGE

Your data is stored securely on Supabase (PostgreSQL) servers in the United States. We use industry-standard encryption for all sensitive data.
